New Mythical Egg Update! 🥚

May 2026 · Smile Games Dev Team

Hey PSX players! 👋 The dev team has been working on something truly massive and we're so excited to finally share all the details with you. This update is packed with new content that we think you're going to absolutely love.

🥚
📸 Replace with image or GIF

🥚 Mythical Eggs Are Here!

We're introducing brand-new Mythical Eggs — the rarest eggs ever added to Pet Simulator X. These eggs contain pets you've never seen before, with completely unique designs, special abilities, and incredible power stats.

  • 🌟 Over 12 brand-new Mythical pets to collect
  • ✨ Every Mythical pet has a Shiny and Rainbow variant
  • 💎 Mythical pets have 5x the stats of regular pets
  • 🏆 Special golden aura exclusive to Mythical owners
✨
📸 Replace with image or GIF

🌎 Three New Worlds

Alongside the new eggs, we've added three entirely new worlds to explore. Each world has its own exclusive egg, hidden chests, and unique environment. Discover the Volcanic Caves, Crystal Sky Islands, and the Deep Ocean Abyss!

  • 🌋 Volcanic Caves — lava-themed pets and fire eggs
  • ☁️ Crystal Sky Islands — floating islands with cloud pets
  • 🌊 Deep Ocean Abyss — deep-sea creatures and glowing pets

🏆 New Leaderboards

We've completely overhauled the leaderboard system! Now you can compete in multiple categories — richest players, most pets hatched, rarest pet owned, and highest damage dealt. Show off your progress and climb the ranks globally!

  • 📊 5 new competitive leaderboard categories
  • 🥇 Weekly reset leaderboards with exclusive prizes
  • 🌍 Global & regional rankings now supported

🐛 Bug Fixes & QoL Improvements

We've also addressed a ton of community-reported issues and snuck in some quality-of-life improvements based on your feedback. Trading is faster, the UI is cleaner, and pets now auto-equip when hatched.
